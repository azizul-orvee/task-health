Common questions

How quickly can care start?

If you need us in a hurry, we can arrange a carer to start in less than 12 hours of you getting in touch — in a crisis, often even quicker.

What if my regular carer is sick or on holiday?

We provide continuity of care. If your carer takes holiday, falls ill — or you simply don’t get on — we’ll provide a replacement.

Do you cover weekends and bank holidays?

Yes. Our office is open Monday to Friday 9:00–18:00, we’re on call at weekends, and you can reach us 24/7 by phone.

Which areas do you cover?

We’re based in Ilford and care for people across Redbridge, East London and Essex — including Barking & Dagenham, Tower Hamlets, Newham, Waltham Forest, Enfield and Dartford — and all over London.

How is care funded?

We can work with private (self-funded) clients, Direct Payments, and care arranged through the Local Authority or NHS. Tell us your situation in the form and we’ll talk you through the options.
