Overview

Caring for a loved one can be deeply rewarding, but it’s also essential for carers to take breaks to recharge and look after themselves. Our respite care offers temporary relief, so family members or primary carers can rest knowing their loved one is in capable hands.

Is Respite Care right for you?

Every situation is different. Talk to our experienced team — we’ll listen first, then suggest the support that fits.

Ask our team or call 020 8594 7282 — 24/7

What’s included

What respite care includes

Every care plan is built around the person — choose the support that matters, and change it as needs change.

  1. 01

    Temporary relief for carers

    A well-deserved break that reduces burnout, so you return feeling refreshed.

  2. 02

    Professional carers

    Highly trained carers step in seamlessly, with the same compassion and expertise.

  3. 03

    Continuity of care

    We learn the person’s routines, preferences and needs for a smooth transition.

  4. 04

    Flexible scheduling

    A few hours, a full day, overnight, or an extended period.

  5. 05

    Companionship & engagement

    Activities that support mental, emotional and physical well-being.

  6. 06

    Help with daily living

    Support with bathing, dressing, grooming and mobility.

  7. 07

    Medication management

    Prescribed medications taken correctly and at the right times.

  8. 08

    Respite at home

    Care in familiar surroundings, for a sense of security and comfort.

  9. 09

    Family education & support

    Resources and information to help families with the challenges of caring.

  10. 10

    Stress reduction for carers

    Regular breaks to protect your own physical and emotional well-being.

  11. 11

    Emergency preparedness

    Carers trained to handle emergencies and keep your loved one safe.

Our promise

We recognise the dedication and commitment of family carers. Our respite care gives you the support and relief you need to keep providing high-quality care to the people you love — while looking after your own well-being too.
