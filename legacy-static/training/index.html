Before they start

An intensive induction for every carer

Each member of staff completes an intensive induction and training programme before they begin working with clients. Our practical sessions cover safeguarding, moving and handling, hoisting equipment and First Aid.

Our team is made up of registered nurses (RGN) and NVQ-trained carers, all taken through theory and practical training in safeguarding, medication, lifting and handling, and using hoisting equipment.

  • Safeguarding
  • Moving & handling
  • Hoisting equipment
  • Medication
  • First Aid
  • Annual refreshers
