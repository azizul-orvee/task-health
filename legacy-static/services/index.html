Live-in carers & companionship care

An alternative to a care home

With a live-in carer you have your own dedicated worker living in your home — so you can stay with your familiar possessions and pets, keep the same daily routines, and friends and family can visit as they normally do.

We provide continuity of care: should your carer take holiday, fall ill or you simply don’t get on, we’ll provide a replacement. We don’t wait for a problem before solving it — we proactively monitor our carers and give ongoing training.

More about live-in care →

What a live-in carer can do

  • Prepare nutritious homemade meals
  • Supervise and prompt medication
  • Wash and dry clothing, bedding and towels
  • Collect prescriptions
  • Housework, bed making and light gardening
  • Collect and deliver allowances and pension benefits
  • Accompany you to hospital and GP appointments
  • Shopping trips, visits and errands
  • Recreational support — social events, hobbies, seeing friends
  • Help looking after pets
  • Showering, bathing, washing, dressing, shaving and oral hygiene
  • Help getting to and from the toilet
  • Moving and transferring in and out of bed

Advanced care

Clients with more specific needs are offered live-in, round-the-clock care at whatever level they require — for example if they:

  • Aren’t mobile on their own and need supervision
  • Need full help with showering, bathing, dressing and oral hygiene
  • Are incontinent and need help maintaining toilet hygiene
  • Are forgetful or repetitive and need constant supervision
  • Need a commode, bottle or catheter bag emptied
  • Cannot be left alone
  • Need help to eat and drink
  • Need prompting with medication or re-ordering prescriptions
  • Live with dementia, Alzheimer’s, Parkinson’s or multiple sclerosis, or need hoisting
