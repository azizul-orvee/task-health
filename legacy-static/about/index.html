Who we are

Home care that puts you in control

The flexibility of home care lets you live safely and comfortably in the home you know best. From daily and weekly care visits right through to the dedicated support of a live-in carer, there are plenty of services available — even on a temporary basis.

You can have a visiting carer, a live-in carer, a sitting carer, a carer to take you shopping, hospital visits, an overnight carer, or a dedicated carer to support all your caring needs and put your mind at ease.

“Task Healthcare supports people to live independently.”

01

Palliative & reablement care

We provide palliative care across London for people living with life-threatening illnesses, and Reablement Care — intensive short-term support to help people relearn daily skills and regain the confidence to live independently.

02

Rooted in our community

We’re based in and around East London and Essex for the most part, and also support clients in Enfield and Dartford. We have strong connections within the local community.

03

Led by experience

Our senior staff are experienced, qualified nurses and carers with 50+ years of combined NHS and community care experience. They’ve seen first-hand every stage of the care journey — and guide our managers and carers every day.

Our aims

What we set out to do

  • Give our patients the best possible care, by understanding and exceeding their expectations.
  • Help people when they need us — treating everyone with compassion, dignity and respect, and taking pride in our work and our organisation.
  • Encourage all team members to take part in achieving our aims and objectives.
  • Always do our very best — learning and innovating wherever we can, and embracing change to enhance our service.
  • Improve satisfaction and experience for all stakeholders.
  • Provide service users with a friendly, quality service in their chosen environment.
  • Promote optimal health, independence and quality living irrespective of age, religion, race, gender, disability, sexuality or belief.
