Overview

During serious illness or at the end of life, comfort, dignity and emotional support become paramount. At Task Healthcare we understand the needs and challenges individuals and families face at these difficult times. Our palliative and end-of-life care is designed to provide specialised, compassionate support that enhances quality of life and ensures a peaceful transition.

Is End-of-Life Care right for you?

Every situation is different. Talk to our experienced team — we’ll listen first, then suggest the support that fits.

How we care at the end of life

Every care plan is built around the person — choose the support that matters, and change it as needs change.

  1. 01

    A holistic approach

    We care for physical needs and for emotional, social and spiritual well-being.

  2. 02

    Pain management

    Working closely with healthcare professionals so pain and discomfort are managed effectively.

  3. 03

    Emotional & psychological support

    Compassionate listening and companionship to ease anxiety and distress.

  4. 04

    Respect for wishes & dignity

    Decisions about treatment, comfort and end-of-life choices are respected.

  5. 05

    Help with daily activities

    Gentle help with daily living, given with sensitivity and respect.

  6. 06

    Family involvement

    Care plans created together with family, in line with the person’s wishes.

  7. 07

    Crisis management

    Experienced carers who support both the individual and their family through crises.

  8. 08

    Spiritual & cultural sensitivity

    Diverse beliefs and traditions are honoured and built into care.

  9. 09

    Legacy & memory care

    Space to share stories, memories and wishes for their legacy.

  10. 10

    Grief support

    Support and resources to help families through the grieving process.

  11. 11

    24/7 availability

    Reassurance and support whenever it’s needed, day or night.

Our promise

Our palliative and end-of-life care brings comfort, dignity and compassionate support at a sensitive time — so individuals and families can focus on cherishing the moments they have together.
