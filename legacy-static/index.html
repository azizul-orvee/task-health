Welcome to Task Health Care

We realise families need issues dealt with quickly and efficiently — and loved ones need to be treated with dignity.

Our Care Coordinator started out as a carer — and climbed the ladder.

Task Healthcare is committed to providing the best health care possible for our clients.

Our senior staff — experienced and qualified nurses and carers — have more than 50 years of combined NHS nursing and community care experience. They’ve seen first-hand what happens at every stage of the care journey, and they use that real-world experience to guide our carers and give you the best possible care and advice.

Services are tailored to each client’s cultural and language needs. From daily or weekly visits through to the dedicated support of a live-in carer — even on a temporary basis — you can shape care around your life, not the other way round.

Common questions

How quickly can care start?

If you need us in a hurry, we can arrange a carer to start in less than 12 hours of you getting in touch — in a crisis, often even quicker.

What if my regular carer is sick or on holiday?

We provide continuity of care. If your carer takes holiday, falls ill — or you simply don’t get on — we’ll provide a replacement.

Do you cover weekends and bank holidays?

Yes. Our office is open Monday to Friday 9:00–18:00, we’re on call at weekends, and you can reach us 24/7 by phone.

Can care respect our culture and language?

Absolutely. Services are tailored to each client’s cultural and language needs, and you can customise care as needs change.

How is care funded?

We can work with private (self-funded) clients, Direct Payments, and care arranged through the Local Authority or NHS. Tell us your situation in the form and we’ll talk you through the options.
