Live-In Care

Round-the-clock care, without leaving home

For people who need continuous support, a compassionate live-in carer brings round-the-clock care and companionship — an alternative to moving into a nursing or residential home.

Overview

For people who need continuous support and assistance, our live-in care provides a compassionate, highly trained carer who lives with the client, ensuring round-the-clock care and companionship. It’s particularly helpful for those with complex needs, or anyone who wants the reassurance of always having someone there. You keep your daily routines, your possessions and your pets — and friends and family can keep visiting as they always have.

Is Live-In Care right for you?

Every situation is different. Talk to our experienced team — we’ll listen first, then suggest the support that fits.

What’s included

What live-in care includes

Every care plan is built around the person — choose the support that matters, and change it as needs change.

  1. 01

    Comprehensive support

    Experienced carers help with personal care, medication management, mobility support and more.

  2. 02

    Personalised care plans

    Every plan is built around the person’s unique needs, preferences and goals.

  3. 03

    24/7 availability

    Your carer is in the home around the clock, ready to help in an emergency or with any other need.

  4. 04

    Companionship

    Conversation and shared activities that promote mental and emotional well-being.

  5. 05

    Privacy & dignity

    Carers trained to give care with the utmost sensitivity and respect.

  6. 06

    Maintaining independence

    Support that preserves a sense of self-reliance and autonomy.

  7. 07

    Household management

    Help with laundry, cleaning, ironing and organising living spaces.

  8. 08

    Nutrition & meal preparation

    Balanced, nutritious homemade meals that respect dietary needs and preferences.

  9. 09

    Safety measures

    Carers who identify and address potential hazards to create a secure home.

  10. 10

    Flexibility in care

    Plans that are adjusted as needs change over time.

  11. 11

    Communication & updates

    Families are kept informed about well-being and any changes to the care plan.

  12. 12

    Continuity of care

    If your carer takes holiday, falls ill — or you simply don’t get on — we provide a replacement.

Our promise

Live-in care gives individuals and families the peace of mind of knowing reliable, compassionate care is always available. Our carers are dedicated to the comfort, safety and well-being of those they support, so people can keep living in the familiarity of their own homes.
