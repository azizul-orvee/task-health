# Task Healthcare — Design Brief

**Concept:** Warm, human home care that feels like family, not a clinic. Calm, confident and trustworthy.

**Audience & primary action:** Adult children arranging care for a parent (and older adults themselves), often under time pressure. Primary action: **request a consultation** (secondary: call, 24/7).

**Direction:** Soft & Organic × Editorial. Generous whitespace, rounded imagery, serif headlines with italic accents.

**Type:** Fraunces (display, soft optical serif) + Plus Jakarta Sans (body). Fluid `clamp()` scale.

**Colour (60/30/10):** warm cream `--cream` dominant · navy ink `--ink` secondary · brand blue `--blue` (from logo, #005FC9) reserved for primary actions and key highlights.

**Motion personality:** gentle and weighty. Slow hero Ken Burns, masked headline reveal, one rotating word, staggered section reveals that play once, count-up stats, 0.97 press feedback. Everything is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Memorable moment:** the hero headline finishes "Compassionate care at home for *your mum / your dad / your partner…*", and the "Who needs care?" chips take you into the consultation form with that answer already filled in.

**Retention hooks:**
- 5-second clarity: outcome headline, proof line, one CTA.
- Authority and social proof: CQC Good, homecare.co.uk 9.9, NHS experience, real reviews placed next to CTAs.
- Commitment and consistency: an easy first tap in the hero carries into the multi-step form.
- Zeigarnik: a 3-step form with a progress bar.
- Progressive disclosure: the full list of aims and the FAQs are accordions.
- Visual rhythm: sections alternate cream, white, navy and blue.
- Fitts: a sticky Call / Consultation bar in the thumb zone on mobile.
